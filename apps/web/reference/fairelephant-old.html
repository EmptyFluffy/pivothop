Methodology

Seven models. One transparent score.

Every input is sourced from public salary, cost-of-living and housing data. Nothing is estimated by hand.

01
Hiring-country baseline
Benchmarks the offer against typical pay for the role in the country doing the hiring.
02
Purchasing power (PPP)
Adjusts the figure so a dollar is measured by what it actually buys where you live.
03
Cost of living
Weighs everyday expenses — food, transport, utilities — against the compensation.
04
Ability to save
Estimates the share of income left after essential spending in your location.
05
Housing affordability
Compares the salary against local rent and ownership costs for a single household.
06
Local salary percentiles
Places the offer on the distribution of comparable local salaries, p10 to p90.
07
Remote market rate
Tracks what comparable fully-remote roles are paying across live job postings.

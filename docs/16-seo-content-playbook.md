# PivotHop — SEO Content Playbook

*The measured version of "write good posts." Compiled July 2026 from ranking-correlation studies and answer-engine research. Every rule below carries its number and its caveat. Re-audit quarterly; the feeds move.*

---

## The evidence base (what correlates with ranking and traffic)

**Length.** Top-ranking pages average ~1,447 words (Backlinko) / ~1,152 (Semrush top performers). The working band for competitive informational queries: **1,500–2,500 words**. Posts over 3,000 words earn ~3.5× more backlinks and ~2.4× more social shares than posts under 1,000. Caveat, stated plainly: this is **correlation, not causation** — long pages rank because thoroughness satisfies intent and earns links, not because word count is a dial. Padding a thin idea to 2,000 words produces a long thin idea.

**Visuals.** The strongest formatting signal in the data: posts with **7+ visuals get ~55% more backlinks and ~116% more organic traffic** than visual-light posts; bloggers using 7–10 visuals report strong results at more than twice the rate of one-visual bloggers. "Visual" ≠ stock photo: tables, data graphics, callout boxes, and pull quotes count as visual breaks. Rule of thumb from readability research: **one visual break every 300–400 words**.

**Scanability.** Bold headings sized ~140% of body let readers scan ~30% faster and retain ~18% more. Bold key phrases and bullet lists act as signposts; scanability → dwell time → the engagement signals rankers reward. Body text 15px+; short paragraphs (2–4 sentences).

**The answer-engine layer (GEO/AEO).** AI Overviews now trigger on ~48% of Google searches; 58–68% of searches end without a click; **brands cited inside AI answers see ~+35% organic CTR**. What earns citations: unique verifiable statistics, 1–2 sentence direct answers under descriptive headers, FAQPage + Article schema, named authors with checkable expertise, dated claims with sources. Only ~11% of domains are cited by both ChatGPT and Perplexity — proprietary data is the moat.

**Freshness.** Visible dates, periodic updates, and "as of [month]" framing on volatile numbers. Stagger publication on a live domain rather than bulk-dropping.

**Fonts.** The research demands legibility (16px-class body, high contrast, real hierarchy), not specific typefaces. Our two-face system (Space Grotesk / Space Mono) satisfies every legibility criterion; we do not add faces for SEO. This is a deliberate, documented deviation from "use system fonts" advice.

## The per-post spec (checklist)

Every PivotHop post ships with:

- [ ] **1,400–2,500 words** of body (data pieces toward the top; essays may run under if the idea is genuinely compact — never pad)
- [ ] **≥5 visual breaks**: tables, bar graphics, callouts, pull quotes (target 7 on pillar pieces)
- [ ] **≥1 data table** with a mono caption naming the source and date
- [ ] **Bold on the load-bearing numbers and claims** (scan the post reading only bold: it should summarize honestly)
- [ ] A **direct answer** in the first 1–2 sentences of the post or of each H3 section
- [ ] **3 FAQ entries** with 1–2 sentence answers (rendered + FAQPage schema)
- [ ] **Article schema**, named author, visible date
- [ ] **Sources-and-method box** with n's, floors, and dated external claims
- [ ] At least one **internal link** to the instrument and one to a sibling post
- [ ] **Zero em dashes; human cadence** (varied sentence length; no listicle voice; a real conclusion under "Where this leaves you")
- [ ] At least one **original statistic no other site can publish** (the citation moat)

## Gap analysis of the first 16 posts (July 2026)

| Criterion | Status before retrofit |
|---|---|
| Unique data, direct answers, FAQs, schema, sources boxes | ✅ already strong |
| Tables | ❌ none — numbers buried in prose |
| Bold signposting | ❌ almost none |
| Visual breaks every 300–400 words | ❌ headers only |
| Bar graphics / callouts / pull quotes | ❌ none |
| Body length | ⚠️ 550–850 words; below the 1,400+ band |
| Internal cross-links between posts | ⚠️ sparse |
| Images (photographic) | ❌ none — by design; CSS data-graphics are our visual layer until an image pipeline exists |

**Retrofit order:** add tables + bold + callouts to the data-heavy posts first (adjacency premium, transferable skills, AI engineer, licensed exits); lengthen only where there is real material; add cross-links opportunistically.

## Voice guardrails (unchanged by any of the above)

Numbers over adjectives. Deadpan. No em dashes. No motivational vocabulary. Bold is a signpost, not shouting: never bold a whole sentence. Tables state sources in their captions. If a visual exists to decorate rather than to carry data, cut it.
